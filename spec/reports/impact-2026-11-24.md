# Impact report — 2026-11-24 (update)

**Mode:** update · **Design baseline:** `f82ec75` → `75e776f`

The design refresh `75e776f` answers the tactical open questions and adds a tactical map generation
passage, based on the Veligrad map tools generator. The spec now has 405 IDs: 13 new, 35 changed, none
removed. 25 of the tactical open questions are resolved, 2 in part, and 4 new ones were added. Every
`/data` table these sections touch is still empty, so no content fails validation.

## What changed in the design

- **Turn frame:**
  - day/night boundary (21:00 is night)
  - the player can interrupt the stall advance
  - a neutral that turns hostile joins the turn order as a third side
  - a disabled leg lowers max stamina
- **Sight and reactions:**
  - drafted sight range, Recon-based spotting and flashlight blinding
  - reaction modifiers
  - take cover: a prone reactor stays prone, and reaction moves trigger no reactions
  - overwatch ends early
- **Hit model:**
  - shooter spread and burst recoil drafted
  - 2σ cone and a statistical shot preview (about 300 samples)
  - weapon condition widens spread
  - jamming and clearing a jam
- **Damage and medical:**
  - blunt damage causes pain only and ignores the falloff floor
  - armour wear per hit, ceramic ×1.5
  - stun 35%
  - the disabling hit's excess is spread too
  - bleeding, fracture and pain chances
  - medical AP floor before surcharges
- **Grenades:** throw distance and scatter; blast cover (full blocks, half halves) and the split over body zones.
- **Objectives and exits:**
  - a VIP is captured only once carried off the map; cargo is saved when it leaves the map
  - request exits are all placed, and generation fills up to 2–4
- **New "Tactical map generation" section:**
  - map types, the 9 stages, buildings from floor-plan rules
  - levels: ground floor and, for large buildings, a roof; a second floor is optional
  - doors (open on move +1 AP, close 1 AP, locked shares), half cover crossed only by vaulting
  - edge destruction by explosives
  - concealment (2 MoA aim shift, spotting ×0.7)
  - optional extras, one random stream per stage
- **Tactical actions and item list:** overwatch, grenade throw, clear a jam, doors, medical AP order; frag blast, ceramic wear, tracer recoil.
