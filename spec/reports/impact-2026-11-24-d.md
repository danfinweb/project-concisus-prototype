## Main design changes

- **Pay and rank:**
  - Each soldier's fee per op (Recruit $300, Regular $600, Veteran $1,200, Elite $2,500, draft) is paid
    when the op launches. No fee, no launch.
  - There is no daily salary, no renewal and no non-payment rule. Ops launch through a new `launchOp`
    management request (MGT-005, STR-028).
  - Rank is the hiring tier. It rises when the average attribute goes above the tier range (45/60/75); it
    never drops (MGT-074).
  - The highest-ranked soldier leads and gives the others a Leadership bonus of +0/+2/+4/+6 to morale and
    reaction chance (MGT-075, TAC-094).
- **Economy:**
  - There is no FOB upkeep. FOBs cost $25,000 in a small city and $50,000 in a large one.
  - The player never repays an advance; a failed contract costs only reputation.
  - Disowned contracts keep their losses, with one disown roll per contract.
  - STR-098 always keeps a low-risk contract posted.
  - Draft prices for ammo, mods and op items. Traders buy gear at 40%, the Krug at 50%.
  - Compensation is $1,000 per soldier killed, at least $2,000. A bounty pay-off is $10,000, or $20,000 at
    reputation −75 or lower.
- **Morale:** kept per soldier in the map request and the battle result (TAC-095). Perk effects in battle
  are drafted (TAC-096). The Nerve effect is ×(1 − Nerve ÷ 200).
- **Gear:**
  - The holster holds a pistol as an extra third weapon that counts toward belt capacity. `swapWeapon` has
    a `slot`.
  - Belt pouch limits are drafted. Diesel canisters refill one fuel unit each.
  - Refilling a magazine with a different load empties it first.
  - Mods list the weapons they fit. Spread and recoil modifiers multiply; ergonomics adds.
  - The recoil modifier range is now ×0.5–1.6. Tracers no longer reveal the shooter.
- **Campaign and strategic:**
  - The reformed ending is a 14-day ceasefire peace and is temporary (GEN-019).
  - Besides the SSV, the VDK and the player, only a rogue PMC can meet the victory rule (`rogueVictory`, a
    loss for the player).
  - The holding-phase patron test uses reputation with the backed state.
  - Rogue raising factors and Krug resentment are drafted.
  - Military POIs are flagged and helicopter damage is defined.
  - Fuel is spent on arrival. One Krug escort per convoy.
  - Leak loot can be sold. A hidden overdraft is used in the scoreTerm test mode.
  - Drafts for POI supply output and event effect sizes.
  - Civilians and the influences are never AI controllers.
- **Tactical:**
  - A fracture alone doesn't lower max stamina.
  - No vaulting while carrying or dragging.
  - TAC Q6, 12, 26, 28 and 36 were already answered by the design text.
- **Tech:** the quality tier is detected at start, and the player can override it.
