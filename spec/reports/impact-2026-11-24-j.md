# Impact report — 2026-11-24 (j): answers to the 18 open questions

**Mode:** update · **Design baseline:** `c1333f3` → `0eec0d4`

The user answered the 18 design questions left open after cleanups (f)–(i). The answers and the new drafts
went into the live docs and then into `design/`. The spec now has 422 IDs: one is new (TAC-097), none were
removed. 12 schemas got a new version. 5 new questions are open, and TAC Q67 is still open.

## Main changes

- **AI soldiers are kept per soldier** (STR Q54):
  - `world` keeps a record for every AI soldier: rank, zone HP, morale, attributes, perks. Attributes and
    perks are a spec choice, added so tie-breaks and checks have a source.
  - The map request copies these records into `units[]`, and battle_result 0.6.0 reports them back in
    `aiUnits[]`.
  - The VIP shock, contamination and off-screen fights all act on the records.
- **Strategic:**
  - A damaged helicopter grounded away from a plant can be field-repaired: 24 h at Technical 50, scaled by
    50 ÷ the repairer's Technical (draft).
  - Tactical-type missions between AI factions count as met when the contractor wins the fight on the
    target's tile.
  - A PMC-held plant pays only its daily output.
  - Brokering the ceasefire takes 24 h (draft).
- **Strategic AI:** a player squad's known objectives come from signed contract missions, revealed to the
  employer or to a faction that sees the squad on the target tile (spec choice; SAI-013, view 0.11.0).
- **Management:**
  - The hire price spreads ±30% within a tier (draft).
  - **Healing** (MGT-010, now "Persistent injuries and recovery"): every hour outside battle, each zone
    heals 1 HP, +1 per 25 of the squad's best Medical, ×2 at an FOB (all drafts). Strategic sends it every
    hour (STR-037).
  - Contamination is spread evenly over the body zones.
  - Perks are granted at hiring.
  - One surviving witness of any faction attributes a war crime to every faction.
  - A failed contract costs the normal "mission failed" loss per open mission.
  - **Krug markets** (MGT-040): each market has its own stock and prices. The stock is a seeded share of the
    Krug item list (60% in a city, 40% in a town), with better items at Krug standing 20+ (drafts).
- **Tactical:**
  - **Demolition charge** (new TAC-097): it goes off only on its placer's remote trigger (`detonateCharge`,
    2 AP draft). Its blast is a frag blast: 100 at the centre, 4 tiles, penetration class 2.
  - A carried body weighs 80 kg (draft) plus its gear.
  - A prone soldier can't fire over half cover; that shot is rejected.
  - Crouched and prone hitbox layouts are drafted. The aiming boxes are now set per stance.
- **Tactical AI:** a hostile armed neutral attacks the side that attacked it and retreats at an acceptable
  loss. Civilians never turn hostile.
