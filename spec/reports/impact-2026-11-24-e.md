# Impact report — 2026-11-24 (e): last open-question decisions

**Mode:** update · **Design baseline:** `06dc2a6` → `c1333f3`

The user answered the 16 open questions from report (d), apart from TEC Q8. The answers and drafts went into
the live docs and then into `design/`. The spec now has 420 IDs: 1 new, none removed. About 20 sections
changed. Two open questions remain: one new and narrow (STR Q53), and TEC Q8, which is outside the spec.

## Main design changes

- **Campaign (GEN):**
  - The victory hold must be unbroken. Losing the capital or dropping below the tile share resets the count
    (GEN-012).
  - If the player's PMC meets the victory rule, that counts as the coup. The holding phase still follows,
    and its success gives `playerCoup` (GEN-012, GEN-014, GEN-015).
  - Foreign PMCs are the player's PMC, Varyag, Aegis and a rogue PMC. The SSV, the VDK and the Krug never
    count (GEN-010).
  - The general ceasefire never starts by itself. Only the player starts it, through the new action
    Broker a ceasefire (STR-099) at a governmental institution, while Trusted with both states and both are
    exhausted (GEN-019). The action's time is not set.
- **Strategic:**
  - A damaged helicopter over water crashes like a downed one. Over land it lands and waits for repair at a
    mechanical plant (STR-034).
  - Compensation and a bounty pay-off are paid remotely, anywhere and at any time (STR-043).
  - A fuel shortage only doubles fuel prices; nobody moves slower (STR-062).
  - STR-098 is confirmed as specced.
  - Refugee recruits get 30% off the fee per op for as long as they serve, at the normal hire price
    (STR-065).
- **Management:**
  - A hired pilot is a normal roster soldier with Technical 50+, paid the normal fee by rank. The separate
    pilot fee and `launchOp.hiredPilot` are removed (MGT-005).
  - The soldier record gets a `feeDiscount` field (MGT-001, MGT-003, MGT-005).
  - Vehicle upkeep is dropped. Plant income is the only recurring booking (MGT-073).
  - Weapon repair drafts: in the field, +2 Condition per hour for every 10 Technical; at a mechanical plant,
    back to 100 at $20 per point (MGT-067, DAT-014).
  - The Leadership bonus goes to the whole squad, the leader included (MGT-075).
- **Data:**
  - Canisters never refuel helicopters (DAT-025, DAT-028).
  - The muzzle `flash` value is removed. Muzzle devices now modify recoil and ergonomics (DAT-016).
  - Helicopter text is aligned with STR-034 and MGT-005 (DAT-028).
  - Vehicles have no upkeep (DAT-024).
- **Tactical:**
  - Drawing the holstered pistol is a swap with a pistol: 2 AP, −1 at Agility 70+ (TAC-042).
  - Casualty morale drafts: ally killed −10, ally wounded −3, enemy killed +3, enemy wounded +1. They apply to
    each soldier with the casualty in line of sight or in its squad (TAC-095).
  - The Leadership bonus includes the leader (TAC-094, TAI text).
  - There is no muzzle-flash reveal.
