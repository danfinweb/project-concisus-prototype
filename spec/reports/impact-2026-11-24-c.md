# Impact report — 2026-11-24 (c): GDD split and open-question decisions

**Mode:** update · **Design baseline:** `3aac08f` → `06e571f`

Two design changes are carried in:

- **The GDD was split.** `design/design.md` now holds only Overview and Factions & campaign. The module text
  moved to six docs: `strategic-layer.md`, `tactical-layer.md`, `management.md`, `ai.md`,
  `data-definitions.md` and `presentation-tech.md`. Every spec Source line and the area table in
  `_index.md` now point to these docs (separate commit). The tactical text moved unchanged.
- **The user decided about 75 open questions.** Many design values marked "open" now have draft numbers.
  The spec names each one as the design's draft default `→ data/...`.

The spec now has 412 IDs: 6 new and 2 removed. About 200 sections changed. Unresolved open questions dropped
from 101 to 21.

## Main design changes

- **Campaign:**
  - A faction wins by holding the capital plus 60% of tiles for 14 days, without a break.
  - The campaign becomes an endless war at Day 120.
  - Cash out is offered above $2M. There is no Krug ending.
  - Coup terms: the approaches, the key regions, 12 soldiers, and "exhausted" at 4 or more crisis signs.
  - The coup needs the final operation to be won. Holding phase: a patron accepts at Friendly, or after 30
    days held; losing the capital fails it.
  - The rogue PMC has a 1% base chance plus 2% per crisis sign of its patron. Its capital rush is the rogue
    event itself.
  - Starting relations, leader effects and Krug unlock levels are drafts.
- **Strategic:**
  - Tiles connect to 4 edge neighbours only.
  - Fuel is a flat amount per tile.
  - Road visibility is removed.
  - A village is a town variant.
  - The vehicle profile is removed (STR-033).
  - Only events that touch a squad interrupt its multi-hour action.
  - A squad on foot has no supplies store.
  - Squads are raised in held towns and cities ($2,000 per soldier, 12 h).
  - The capital holder gets the leader seat and +$3,000 per day.
  - Contamination costs 2 HP per soldier per hour (STR-097).
  - The Krug escort is hired at a Krug market for $200 + $50 per tile, and the Krug AI may refuse
    (SAI-017).
  - Faction funds: the full contract pay is needed to post a contract; repayments are credited to the faction;
    an employer default costs no reputation.
  - Drafts for action times, evasion, the Krug pass, auto-resolve, trigger thresholds and measure durations.
  - Flavour events have a 0.5% daily chance.
- **Strategic AI:**
  - Resistance groups and the rogue PMC get their own controller and doctrine.
  - The Unfriendly rule holds.
  - Aegis learning is optional.
  - Drafts for the doctrine characters.
- **Management:**
  - Morale is kept per soldier.
  - Reputation is gained per mission, by pay band.
  - Intel needs alone can create offers.
  - A disowned contract is still paid, but gives no reputation.
  - If a rival is first, the mission fails with no reputation loss.
  - Any survivor is a witness.
  - The stash is per FOB.
  - The balance never goes negative.
  - Strategic rolls the fate of soldiers left behind; MIA soldiers may return.
  - The economy rules cover every cost and income (MGT-073).
  - Mission shares are split by pay.
  - A contract fails at its deadline or when all its missions fail.
  - Modding works anywhere outside battle.
  - Leadership comes from rank only.
  - Callsign tie, deed and used-up list rules.
  - Drafts for tiers, perks, growth, insurance, scav loadout, prices and score.
- **Items and data:**
  - Trader prices (ITM-034) and the magazine table.
  - Medical weights; the medkit heals 40 HP per use.
  - The holster holds the sidearm; belt limits, with at most 2 grenades on a belt.
  - The magazine mod slot is removed; tactical mods may carry small modifiers.
  - Magazines are refilled only between fights, with one load type.
  - Loot location tags.
  - Drafts for vehicle values.
- **Tactical:**
  - In a reaction triggered by a visible action in place, the acting soldier counts as the mover
    (TAC-022, Q57).
  - Text aligned with the item drafts: ammo spread, belt grenades, medkit HP (TAC-041/043/044/066).
- **Tech:**
  - Preact + htm; GitHub Pages hosting.
  - Ironman or save-anywhere, chosen per campaign.
  - Save export/import (TEC-031) replaces the cloud save (TEC-029, removed).
  - `SAVE_VERSION` migrations (TEC-032).
  - The slice has 18 weapons.
  - Drafts for the UI values.
