# Untitled Tactical RPG – Strategic actions

Nov 22, 2026 · @Dan

**Draft — not final.** Every action the player can take on the strategic map, with the game time it takes and its other costs, collected from the Strategic Layer and Management sections of the [Game Design Document](https://claude.ai/artifact/EntHEaJm8q32rPTHWXMbvo). Time runs in full hours only, and for every hour that passes every faction takes one action of its own. "Open" marks a value not set yet.

## Movement and operations

| Action | Where | Time | Other cost | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch an op (leave an FOB) | Any FOB | Not stated | Squad, loadout and vehicle committed | Time open |
| Move one region by ground vehicle | Any passable region | 1 hour on roads, 2 on steppe, 3 in woods; mountains by road only; water only at bridges and fords | Fuel (vehicle fuel range); off-road rating lowers the time in steppe and woods | Defined |
| Move one region on foot | Any passable region | 2 hours on most ground, 3 in mountains | — | Defined |
| Fly one region by helicopter | Anywhere | 1 hour, any terrain | Fuel; needs a pilot with Technical 50+ or a hired pilot; noticed by every faction in range | Defined |
| Land a helicopter | Any open ground except water; the only way into hidden cities | Not stated | — | Open |
| Enter a point of interest | The region's point of interest | Not stated | — | Open |
| Scout | Current region | 3 hours (placeholder) | — | Defined |
| Wait | Anywhere | As set by the player | — | Open (Rest covers set hours) |
| Rest | Anywhere; healing is faster at an FOB | As many hours as the player sets | — | Defined |
| Fight a tactical engagement | Where squads meet and the encounter turns hostile | Always 1 hour | Ammunition, wounds, gear at risk | Defined |
| Evade an encounter | Where squads meet | Not stated | Chance from Recon and terrain | Open |
| Pay your way through (Krug) | Encounter with a Krug squad | Not stated | Money | Open |
| Station a squad at a plant | A plant the PMC has taken | Not stated | The squad stays to hold it; output goes to the nearest FOB daily | Open |
| Return to an FOB (end an op) | Any FOB | Arrival ends the op | — | Defined |

## Trade, services, contracts and factions

| Action | Where | Time | Other cost | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trade (buy, sell, barter) | Faction traders in cities, Krug markets | No time | Money or goods; stock depends on reputation; barter deals refresh daily at 00:00 | Defined |
| Hire soldiers | FOBs; local recruits in towns and Krug markets | No time | Salary by price tier | Defined |
| Buy or sell a vehicle | Large cities, mechanical plants; some only with high standing or through the Krug | No time (trade) | Vehicle price | Defined |
| Accept a contract | FOB, cities, Krug markets (Krug work) | Not stated | Filtered by reputation with the employer; advance paid on signing | Open |
| Hand over items or a VIP (contract delivery) | The contract's destination | Not stated | — | Open |
| Hand in faction or operation items | A point of interest held by that faction, or a Krug market | One player action (time not stated) | Gives money and reputation | Open |
| Buy intel on a VIP | Factions hostile to the VIP's side (standing ≥ 20, leader ≥ 50); Krug markets for goods | Not stated | Major $2,000, Colonel $5,000, General $12,000, leader $30,000 (Krug: goods of a lower value) | Time open |
| Hospital treatment | Small and large cities | Not stated | Paid | Open |
| Vehicle repairs | Mechanical plants | Not stated | Paid | Open |
| Refuel | Gas stations, oil plants; helicopters only at FOBs and oil plants | Not stated | Paid | Open |
| Buy a new FOB | Small and large cities | Not stated | Price open | Open |
| Take over an FOB site | By force, through an op | As the op | — | Open |
| Pay compensation (Operation dispute) | The hurt faction | Not stated | Money | Open |
| Pay off a bounty | The faction that set it | Not stated | Money | Open |
